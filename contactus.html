Frequently Asked Questions

You can check your detailed lecture and lab attendance records by clicking on the Attendance tab in the navigation menu or sidebar drawer. Your percentage and eligibility status are updated daily.

Navigate to the Assignments section. Here you can view pending assignments, due dates, submission status, and directly upload your solution files through the upload form.

Click the Dark Mode / Light Mode toggle button located in the top-right corner of the header. Your selected theme preference will be automatically saved in your browser (localStorage) and applied across all pages.

For class schedule conflicts or elective queries, please consult your department academic coordinator or send an inquiry using the Contact Us form above.
